for you
sometimes I wonder
how one person can quietly
become such a beautiful part
of someone else's life.

and then I think of you,
and somehow the answer
becomes very simple.

you are the kind of person
I never want to take for granted,
the kind I want to understand
even on the days you don't
understand yourself.

I may not always have
the perfect words,
but I hope you always know
that somewhere in this world
there is a heart that chooses you,
cares for you,
and quietly wishes
to see you happy.

and if you ever forget
how special you are,
come back here.

I'll remind you. ♡
